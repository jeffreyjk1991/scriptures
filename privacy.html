Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Saved passages and preferences

In the current version of WisdomScroll, saved passages and language preferences are stored locally in your browser on your device. They are not tied to an account.

Audio

The Listen feature uses speech synthesis available through your browser or device. Voice availability depends on the languages and voices installed on your device.

Analytics and future services

If analytics, accounts or additional services are added later, this policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and why.

External links

Guide and source pages may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact method published on the site once the public launch contact address is finalized.
