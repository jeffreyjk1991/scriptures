Selected Guru Granth Sahib passages

There is but one God, whose name is True, the Creator, devoid of fear and enmity.
Mool Mantar

The opening creed describes the oneness and attributes of the Divine.

How shall man become true before God? How shall the veil of falsehood be rent?
Japji Sahib 1

Guru Nanak poses the question of truthful living.

By walking, O Nanak, according to the will of the Commander as pre-ordained.
Japji Sahib 1 — Hukam

The answer points toward living in harmony with hukam.

Gurmukhi text and English translation

The Guru Granth Sahib is written in Gurmukhi script and contains compositions in several languages and linguistic forms. Scroll My Faith keeps the Gurmukhi text and English rendering clearly labeled. Hindi is currently available only on selected passages, so unavailable passages are not silently replaced with English.

Read with context

These short Guru Granth Sahib verses are for discovery, not a replacement for the complete scripture or Sikh interpretive tradition. Use the Study control for immediate context and the beginner guide for background on the text.
