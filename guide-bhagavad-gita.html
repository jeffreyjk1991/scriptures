What is the Bhagavad Gita?

The Bhagavad Gita is a Sanskrit dialogue within the Mahabharata. It presents a conversation between the warrior Arjuna and Krishna at a moment of profound moral conflict.

What is it about?

Readers encounter questions about duty, action, detachment, devotion, knowledge, self-mastery and the nature of reality. The text is not simply a collection of inspirational quotations; its teachings unfold within a specific philosophical and narrative setting.

Why read it in Sanskrit or Hindi?

Sanskrit preserves the source language. Hindi can make the text more accessible for many readers in India, while English remains useful for a global audience. Scroll My Faith keeps the language selector separate from the app interface so readers can choose the scripture language they prefer.
