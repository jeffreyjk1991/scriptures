What is the Dhammapada?

The Dhammapada is a well-known collection of Buddhist verses preserved in the Pāli Canon. Its short teachings often address mind, intention, conduct, anger, discipline and liberation.

Why it works well in a scroll format

The text contains many concise verses, but context still matters. WisdomScroll combines a quick reading experience with a Study control so the feed can be an entry point rather than the end of learning.

Pāli and translation

Pāli preserves an early canonical language of Theravāda Buddhism. English and local-language translations can help readers understand the teachings while the source-language option remains available.
