What is the Tao Te Ching?

The Tao Te Ching is a foundational text associated with Taoist thought and traditionally attributed to Laozi. Its short chapters explore the Tao, simplicity, humility, non-forcing and natural order.

Why translations differ

Classical Chinese is compact and often permits more than one interpretation. English translations can therefore vary substantially. Scroll My Faith labels the translation and provides Chinese text where available.

Reading beyond quotations

Its memorable lines are easy to isolate, but the chapters develop ideas through paradox, imagery and contrast. Context remains important even in a short-form reader.
