What is the Guru Granth Sahib?

The Guru Granth Sahib is the central scripture of Sikhism and is revered by Sikhs as the eternal Guru. It contains devotional compositions by Sikh Gurus as well as other contributors whose hymns are included in the Sikh canon.

What does “Guru Granth Sahib” mean?

The title combines Guru, Granth (a book or scriptural volume) and Sahib, an honorific of respect. In Sikh practice, however, the Guru Granth Sahib is not treated as merely a book: it holds the role of the eternal Guru.

Gurmukhi, language and translation

The scripture is written in Gurmukhi script, while its compositions draw on several languages and linguistic forms. English translation can make the text accessible to more readers, but translation cannot fully preserve every poetic, musical and interpretive nuance.

How to read it respectfully

Short passages can introduce themes such as truthful living, hukam, remembrance, humility and devotion. They are best treated as entry points into the larger Sikh scriptural and interpretive tradition.
