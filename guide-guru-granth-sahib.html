What is the Guru Granth Sahib?

The Guru Granth Sahib is the central scripture of Sikhism and is revered by Sikhs as the eternal Guru. It contains devotional compositions by Sikh Gurus and other contributors.

Gurmukhi and translation

The scripture is commonly encountered in Gurmukhi script. Translation can improve accessibility, but the original language, musical form and interpretive tradition remain important to understanding the text.
