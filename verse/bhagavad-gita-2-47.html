Bhagavad Gita 2:47

Thy business is with the action only, never with its fruits.

Sanskrit: कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।

Hindi: तुम्हारा अधिकार केवल कर्म करने में है, उसके फलों में कभी नहीं।

What does Bhagavad Gita 2:47 mean?

In this passage Krishna directs Arjuna toward disciplined action without making attachment to the result the basis of action. The verse is often summarized as focusing on the work rather than the reward, but the surrounding dialogue is broader: it concerns duty, judgment, self-mastery and the relationship between action and attachment.

Context

Bhagavad Gita 2:47 appears during Krishna’s response to Arjuna’s crisis about whether and how to act. It is not a teaching that outcomes never matter; it addresses how a person relates inwardly to action and its fruits.
