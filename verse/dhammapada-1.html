Dhammapada 1

All that we are is the result of what we have thought.

Pāli: Manopubbaṅgamā dhammā, manoseṭṭhā manomayā.

Hindi: हम जो कुछ हैं, वह हमारे विचारों का परिणाम है।

What is Dhammapada 1 about?

The opening verse emphasizes the formative role of mind and intention. In the Dhammapada, mental states are closely connected with speech, action and the consequences that follow from them.

Context

The Dhammapada belongs to the Khuddaka Nikāya of the Pāli Canon. Its short verses are memorable, but traditional interpretation reads them within a wider Buddhist framework of intention, conduct and mental cultivation.
