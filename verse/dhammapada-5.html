Dhammapada 5

Hatred does not cease by hatred at any time: hatred ceases by love.

Pāli: Na hi verena verāni sammantīdha kudācanaṃ; averena ca sammanti.

Hindi: घृणा से घृणा कभी समाप्त नहीं होती; प्रेम से ही समाप्त होती है।

What does Dhammapada 5 mean?

The verse teaches that hostility is not ended by answering hostility with more hostility. The Pāli emphasizes avera, non-hatred or freedom from enmity, as the alternative that interrupts the cycle.

Context

This is one of the Dhammapada’s best-known teachings on anger and conflict. It is useful as a short reflection, but its fuller meaning sits within Buddhist teachings on intention, conduct and liberation from harmful mental states.
