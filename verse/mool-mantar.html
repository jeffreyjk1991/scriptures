Mool Mantar

Short answer: The Mool Mantar is the opening statement of Sikh scripture and presents foundational descriptions of the Divine: oneness, truth, creative presence, fearlessness, absence of enmity, timelessness, unborn nature and self-existence.

Scroll excerpt — Macauliffe-era public-domain English:

There is but one God, whose name is True, the Creator, devoid of fear and enmity.

Complete Gurmukhi line:
ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ ਅਕਾਲ ਮੂਰਤਿ ਅਜੂਨੀ ਸੈਭੰ ਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਸਾਦਿ ॥

Common Romanization: Ik Oankar Sat Nam Karta Purakh Nirbhau Nirvair Akaal Moorat Ajooni Saibhang Gur Prasad.

Selected Hindi rendering: एक परम सत्य है; उसका नाम सत्य है, वह सृष्टिकर्ता है, निर्भय और निर्वैर है।

The English and Hindi lines in the scroll are short excerpts. The complete Gurmukhi line above includes the remaining attributes through Gur Prasad.

What is the Mool Mantar?

The Mool Mantar opens Sikh scripture and introduces foundational descriptions of the Divine, including oneness, truth, creative presence, fearlessness and absence of enmity. The short rendering above is the public-domain English wording used in Scroll My Faith’s current corpus.

Why context matters

The Mool Mantar is central to Sikh thought and devotional life. A short translation cannot carry every nuance of the Gurmukhi and the broader Sikh interpretive tradition, so the original script and source context should remain visible alongside any English rendering.

Source and translation note

English renderings of the Mool Mantar vary because several Gurmukhi terms carry interpretive depth. Scroll My Faith keeps the original line visible and labels its historical English source. For authoritative background, see the SGPC overview of Sri Guru Granth Sahib.
