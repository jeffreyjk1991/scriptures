Mool Mantar

There is but one God, whose name is True, the Creator, devoid of fear and enmity.

Gurmukhi: ੴ ਸਤਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖੁ ਨਿਰਭਉ ਨਿਰਵੈਰੁ

Hindi: एक परम सत्य है; उसका नाम सत्य है, वह सृष्टिकर्ता है, निर्भय और निर्वैर है।

What is the Mool Mantar?

The Mool Mantar opens Sikh scripture and introduces foundational descriptions of the Divine, including oneness, truth, creative presence, fearlessness and absence of enmity. The short rendering above is the public-domain English wording used in Scroll My Faith’s current corpus.

Why context matters

The Mool Mantar is central to Sikh thought and devotional life. A short translation cannot carry every nuance of the Gurmukhi and the broader Sikh interpretive tradition, so the original script and source context should remain visible alongside any English rendering.
