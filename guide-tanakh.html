What is the Tanakh?

Tanakh is a common Jewish name for the Hebrew Bible. The name reflects its three major divisions: Torah, Nevi’im (Prophets) and Ketuvim (Writings).

Hebrew and translation

The Hebrew text carries linguistic detail that translations may render differently. Scroll My Faith offers Hebrew where available alongside a labeled English public-domain translation.

Reading responsibly

Jewish interpretation has a long tradition of commentary and debate. A scrolling verse can be a starting point, but not a substitute for the larger textual and interpretive context.
