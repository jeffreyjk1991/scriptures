Scriptures for Grief and Loss Across Faith Traditions

A multi-faith guide to scripture about grief, loss, hardship and comfort across the Bible, Qur’an, Tanakh, Buddhist and other traditions.

Multi-faith scriptureEvergreen guide

Why people turn to scripture in grief

Grief often creates questions that practical advice cannot answer: what does suffering mean, how should a person endure loss, and where can comfort be found? Religious traditions answer these questions in different ways.

Scroll My Faith groups passages by theme to help readers discover those differences, not erase them.

Different traditions frame grief differently

Biblical psalms often give grief a voice through lament, trust and prayer. The Qur’an frequently connects hardship with patience, responsibility and trust in God. Buddhist texts may emphasize impermanence, attachment and the cultivation of the mind.

These are not interchangeable teachings. The value of a multi-faith view is that each tradition can be encountered on its own terms.

How to use a short scripture feed responsibly

A short passage can be comforting, but grief is rarely resolved by a quotation. Use scripture as a starting point for reflection, community, prayer, study or conversation with a trusted religious leader when appropriate.
