Scriptures About Forgiveness Across Religions

Explore how different religious traditions speak about forgiveness, mercy, reconciliation and overcoming hostility.

Multi-faith scriptureEvergreen guide

Forgiveness is not one identical idea

Christianity, Judaism, Islam, Buddhism, Hindu traditions and Sikh teachings all include ideas related to mercy, reconciliation, compassion or overcoming hostility, but those ideas sit inside different theological frameworks.

A useful comparison begins by preserving those differences rather than treating all passages as saying the same thing.

Themes that often appear

Some passages focus on mercy. Others focus on refusing revenge, overcoming hatred, making peace or acting without hostility. The moral vocabulary may overlap while the religious explanation behind it differs.

Use comparison as a doorway, not a conclusion

A themed page can help a reader discover a passage they would never otherwise encounter. The next step is to read the larger source and learn how that tradition interprets the passage.
