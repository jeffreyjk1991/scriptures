About Scroll My Faith

Scroll My Faith is a calm, mobile-first way to encounter scripture and wisdom texts from major religious traditions. The home feed intentionally mixes traditions, while every passage remains clearly labeled so the source is never hidden.

Every tradition, in its own words

We do not claim that religions teach the same thing. Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, Judaism, Sikhism and Taoism have distinct histories, doctrines, practices and scriptures. Scroll My Faith preserves those distinctions while making discovery easier.

Why a scroll?

Traditional scripture websites are often built like reference libraries. Scroll My Faith uses a familiar vertical reading pattern so someone can begin reading immediately, then choose to study context, save a passage, listen to it or filter to a specific tradition.

Languages

English is the default. Where available, the reader can switch to original or locally important languages such as Arabic, Hindi, Sanskrit, Punjabi, Hebrew and Chinese. Translation and source information is shown alongside the passage.
