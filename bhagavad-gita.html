What is the Bhagavad Gita?

The Bhagavad Gita is a Sanskrit dialogue within the Mahabharata. Set during Arjuna’s moral crisis before battle, it explores duty, action, detachment, devotion, knowledge, self-mastery and the nature of reality. Scroll My Faith presents short passages for discovery while keeping the citation and source visible.

English, Sanskrit and selected Hindi

English is available throughout the Gita collection and Sanskrit is available across most of the short passages. Hindi is currently a limited preview on selected verses, so the site does not silently substitute English when a Hindi passage is unavailable.

Read with context

Short verses are useful entry points, but the Bhagavad Gita is a connected dialogue rather than a quotation collection. Use the Study control for immediate context, then follow the source when you want the larger argument around a verse.
