Compare short scriptures about compassion, kindness and care across seven major faith traditions.

How religions approach compassion

Compassion can be expressed as mercy, kindness, loving concern, non-harm, service, generosity or care for the vulnerable. Those ideas overlap in places but arise from distinct scriptural and doctrinal settings.

This comparison is designed to help readers notice both resonance and difference while keeping every passage attributed to its own source.
