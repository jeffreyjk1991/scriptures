How religions approach love

Love can mean devotion, mercy, compassion, goodwill, fidelity, service or care for others depending on the tradition and passage. A Christian teaching about love of neighbor, a Buddhist teaching about non-hatred, a Sikh teaching about devotion, or a Taoist teaching about compassion should not be treated as interchangeable simply because they can all be grouped under the English word “love.”

This page lets you compare those passages side by side while preserving their citations and traditions.
