How religions approach peace

“Peace” is not a single identical concept across religions. Christian and Jewish passages may frame it through trust, justice and reconciliation; Qur’anic passages often connect peace with patience, remembrance and right conduct; Hindu and Buddhist texts frequently emphasize steadiness of mind, non-hatred and disciplined action; Sikh scripture speaks to truthful living, humility and divine remembrance; Taoist texts often approach harmony through simplicity and non-forcing.
