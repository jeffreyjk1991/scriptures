How religions approach hope and trust

Hope and trust appear through different religious vocabularies: reliance on God, patience through hardship, refuge, steadiness, disciplined practice, confidence in right action, remembrance and acceptance of what cannot be forced. The traditions represented here do not define faith or trust in the same way.
