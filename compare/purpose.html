How religions approach purpose

Religious traditions answer questions of purpose in different ways. Some passages emphasize divine calling or obedience, others duty and right action, ethical responsibility, self-knowledge, disciplined practice, remembrance or alignment with the Way. Scroll My Faith keeps those differences visible instead of presenting one universal doctrine of purpose.
