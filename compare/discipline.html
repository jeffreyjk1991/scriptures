How religions approach discipline

Discipline may appear as self-control, prayer, patience, duty, meditation, ethical practice, remembrance, humility or even restraint from forcing action. Because these ideas come from different theological and philosophical systems, Scroll My Faith compares passages without claiming that they teach one common method.
